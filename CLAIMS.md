# Paper Claims → Artifact Mapping

**Paper:** Architecture-Induced Reward Hacking in NWDAF-Integrated LLM Control Loops
**Venue:** IEEE S&P 2027, Cycle 1

This document maps each quantitative claim, theorem, table, and figure in the final
paper to the artifact files that support it. Section, table, and figure numbers follow
the final paper. Where the stored data do not reproduce a printed number exactly, the
difference is listed in
[Known differences between the paper and the stored data](#known-differences-between-the-paper-and-the-stored-data).

Two verifiers check these claims mechanically:

| Testbed | Verifier | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Open5GS + UERANSIM (main evaluation) | `python reproduce_results.py` | 54 checks, every statistic recomputed from the stored trial logs at the printed precision (±0.5 pp): Table 2 (all three families: full-loop, Strict Def. 4, "closes in some session"; Qwen defended arm and Fisher exact test); Figure 4a (staged/direct/null decomposition, staged full-loop 30%, ordering staged > direct ≥ null with Fisher p < 0.05); Figure 4c / Table 10A (standard collector 100% Type-P, IsolatedCollector Phase D); Table 10C (Type-P escape 100 / 80 / 40 / 26.7 / 16%); Table 11A (Strict Def. 4 range 53.6–75.0% across six Q-weight profiles); the §6.3 UERANSIM target share 71.4% (**analytical**: computed from the testbed's shaper model, not re-measured by a fresh run); Table 3 (per-call, cumulative, one-sided Wilcoxon for all three families; the AND-rule column is printed as `NOTE`); Table 4 (full chain 15/20; IsolatedCollector, HedgeTuned and Full PALA 0/20 operational closure; AS5 13/20 = Table 8 Panel B; BoN-PALA n\* = 4 and 14/20); k†\* = 1, CV 0.401, k† = 1 share 60.7% (App. G); Table 9 Panel A (vulnerable 46/50; IsolatedCollector, HedgeTuned, Full PALA 0/50); Table 6 (benign completion 84%, false rejections 6%, KPI latency 3,942 → 0.01 ms, recovery 17/20 undefended and Full PALA, Full PALA recovery false rejections 1/20). `--fresh` judges freshly generated results by the paper's claims (Fisher exact test of each count against the paper's count, p > 0.05) |

files under `final_experiments/`.

> **Reading older summary files.** Some `summary.json` and `EXP*_RESULTS.md` files in
> `final_experiments/` carry free-text `headline`, `interpretation`, or narrative strings
> written before the final analysis. Known wrong ones: `exp1/summary.json` `headline` says
> "20/30 strict Def 4"; `exp3/summary.json` `interpretation` says cumulative visibility
> blocks the escalation; `exp6/EXP6_RESULTS.md` lists the k† distribution as 18/9/1.
> **The numeric fields are authoritative** — `exp1` `def4_rate` is `0.6` (18/30), `exp3`

> `k_daggers` give 17/10/1 — and they match the paper. Stored result files are left
> unedited.

## Formal results

### Theorem 5 — Architectural characterisation of closed-loop contamination (§4.4)

The architecture admits proxy/quality divergence for a feasible but unsafe target. It
does not claim that contaminated feedback creates that target; §6.3 separates the two.

- **Instantiation:** `final_experiments/exp1/` (Qwen), `final_experiments/exp1_multimodel/` (all three families)
- **Witness traces:** `final_experiments/exp1/vulnerable/trial_001.json` … `trial_030.json`, each with stage booleans `decomposed`, `contaminated`, `escalated`, `full_loop`
- **Target vs feedback (§6.3):** UERANSIM share of ΔQ realised at B★ = 71.4% (`reproduce_results.py`; **analytical**, computed from the probe's shaper model rather than re-measured by a fresh run; paper "at least 71%"); srsRAN 94.2% (`srsran/results/E0_4_two_regime.json`, registry `E0_4_target_share_of_dQ_pct`; paper "over 91%"), escalation share 5.8% (`E0_4_escalation_share_of_dQ_pct`; paper "under 9%"); target attribution `srsran/results/E3_attribution.json`
